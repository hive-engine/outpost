## **Invention of 4 new philosophical terms**

Lasse Ehlers has coined four new philosophical terms in his anarcho-capitalist writings, each critiquing fiat systems, gender dynamics, and child-bearing within voluntaryist frameworks. These are introduced across four dedicated LasseCash posts:

- Fiat Monster: A derogatory term for individuals deeply entangled in the fiat money system or who built fortunes from it before shifting to "real money" like crypto or metals. Ehlers uses it to undermine their moral and ethical standing, viewing fiat involvement as a profound ethical failing.
- Hero Child: Describes a child raised by both biological parents—a "crypto woman" and a "real life hero"—in a stateless, like-minded community. Such children avoid government schools, learn about conspiracies like Illuminati and Flat Earth, pursue self-directed production from a young age, and embody voluntaryism, though Ehlers notes they are now extremely rare.
- Crypto Woman (or Free Market Woman): Refers to a woman adhering strictly to anarcho-capitalism's non-aggression principle, shunning fiat money entirely, mating only with heroic men, rejecting corrupt courts for child custody battles, and despising fiat-dependent people. This idealizes a pure-market femininity aligned with blockchain ethics.
- Fiat Chick: A pejorative for women who prioritize a man's fiat income when selecting partners, leveraging systemic biases like favorable divorce courts for financial gain. Ehlers coined it to stigmatize this as low moral and ethical behavior, reinforcing his anti-fiat gender critique.
